Tama Live – Child Safety Policy

Effective Date: 23 November 2026
Last Updated: 23 November 2026

Tama Live is committed to maintaining a safe platform and protecting children from Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, and any activity that puts children at risk.

1. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

Tama Live strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form. Users must not create, upload, share, distribute, request, promote, or access Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Prohibited activities include:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.
  • Grooming or sexual solicitation of children.
  • Sexual extortion or trafficking involving children.
  • Sharing links or content that sexually exploits minors.
  • Any attempt to use live streaming, chat, audio rooms, or other features to endanger children.

2. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report accounts, profiles, live streams, messages, or other content that may involve child exploitation through the reporting mechanism available within the Tama Live app. Users may also contact our Child Safety Team by email.

When reporting, please provide relevant details such as:

  • Username or account ID.
  • Room or content information.
  • Date and approximate time of the incident.
  • A brief description of the concern.

Do not download, copy, forward, or redistribute suspected CSAM.

3. Investigation and Enforcement

Tama Live reviews child safety reports and takes appropriate action in accordance with this policy and applicable laws.

Enforcement actions may include:

  • Removing violating content.
  • Suspending or permanently terminating user accounts.
  • Restricting access to platform features.
  • Taking urgent action to protect users.
  • Reporting confirmed CSAM to the appropriate authorities or designated child protection organizations, as required by applicable law.

4. Child Safety Prevention

We work to prevent child exploitation through platform rules, user reporting mechanisms, content moderation, and enforcement against accounts that violate our safety standards. We may update our safety measures as necessary.

5. Compliance with Child Safety Laws

Tama Live is committed to complying with applicable child protection and child safety laws and regulations. We will cooperate with appropriate law enforcement agencies and relevant child protection authorities when legally required.

6. Child Safety Point of Contact

For child safety concerns, suspected exploitation, reports of CSAM, or inquiries from Google Play regarding our child safety practices, please contact our designated Child Safety Contact.

7. Policy Updates

Tama Live may update this Child Safety Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our platform, safety procedures, or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will always be published on this page.
